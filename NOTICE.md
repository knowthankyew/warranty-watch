## 1. Statutory Educational Tool Notice (Not Legal Advice)
WarrantyWatch is an educational statutory reality engine analyzing consumer product warranties against the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act and state commercial code provisions. It does not provide legal advice.

## 2. Consumer Privacy Defaults & Air-Gap Invariant
By default, all warranty parsing and statute audits execute 100% locally in-browser with zero remote network egress.

## 3. Software Bill of Materials (SBOM)
A complete, machine-readable CycloneDX Software Bill of Materials (`bom.json`) is generated on build in CI via OWASP `@cyclonedx/cdxgen` and attached to release artifacts.
